# Code of Conduct

## Our Commitment

We are committed to providing a welcoming, respectful, and harassment-free environment for everyone participating in this project, regardless of background or level of experience.

## Expected Behavior

- Be respectful and constructive in comments, code reviews, and discussions.
- Focus on what is best for the community and project.
- Gracefully accept constructive feedback.

## Unacceptable Behavior

Harassment, derogatory comments, personal attacks, or any form of disrespectful conduct will not be tolerated.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers reserve the right to remove comments, close issues, or block individuals who do not adhere to these basic standards.
